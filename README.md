## **The Lunge Mine (Shitotsubakurai)**

Bring the World War II Japanese weapon, the "Lunge Mine," into your Minecraft world! In this mod, it's recreated as a highly unstable, high-risk, high-reward (well, not really) weapon. Whether you use it to deal with monsters (which I assume you won't want to), or for "friendly interactions" with your friends (which I assume you definitely will), it gets the job done perfectly.

The mod features a custom model made with Blockbench, along with special charging voice lines, bringing you a unique (and utterly chaotic) gaming experience.

### **Features & Gameplay**
* **Crafting Recipe:** Place **1 TNT** and **2 Sticks** diagonally in a crafting table.
* **The "Charge" Mechanic (Right-Click):** * Hold right-click to initiate the charge! You will be granted **Speed VI** for 5 seconds.
    * During the charge, your keyboard movement is locked (forcing you to dash straight forward), but you can still steer and change your perspective using your mouse.
    * **Detonation:** Colliding with any entity, the ground, or a wall during the charge will instantly trigger an explosion.
    * **The Cost:** The weapon is consumed upon detonation. The user will take **16 points of damage**, while surrounding entities and terrain will suffer normal explosion damage and destruction.

## **二战日军刺雷 (Lunge Mine)**

将二战历史中日军的武器“刺雷”带入你的 Minecraft 世界！在这个模组中，它被还原成了一把极具不稳定性、高风险,高回报(其实并不)的武器。无论是用来对付怪物（我想你不会希望的），还是用来和朋友“友好互动”（我想你会希望的），它都能胜任。

模组包含了使用 Blockbench 制作的模型，还有特殊的冲锋语音，为你带来独特（且极其混乱）的游戏体验。

### **主要玩法与机制**
* **合成配方：** 在工作台中将 **1个 TNT** 和 **2根木棍** 呈对角线斜向摆放即可合成。
* **冲锋机制（右键）：** * 长按右键即可发起冲锋！玩家将获得持续 5 秒的 **速度 VI** 效果。
    * 在冲锋期间，键盘的方向控制将被锁定（强制直线向前冲），但你仍可以通过滑动鼠标来改变冲锋视角和方向。
    * **引爆：** 在冲锋过程中撞击到任何生物、地面或墙体，都会瞬间触发爆炸。
    * **代价：** 触发爆炸后武器将被消耗。使用者将承受 **16点伤害**，而周围的生物和地形则会承受正常的爆炸伤害和破坏。

### **极度不稳定（隐藏机制！）**
